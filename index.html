- output beyond the realm of peer review

I'm Filip. I'm a PhD candidate in the Dynamical Neuroscience program at UCSB.

I work in Spencer LaVere Smith's lab, where we build multiphoton imaging hardware to answer questions about neuronal computation in mouse cortex.

My main project is to add holographic optomanipulation functionality to Diesel2p, enabling single-cell read and write capability over a 25 mm² plane of mouse cortex.

I found my way into tool development through in vivo testing of biological sensors during my time at Janelia with Eric Schreiter and Kaspar Podgorski (2019–2021).

Today, I get to work with Che-Hang Yu and the rest of the lab developing large FOV optical systems for neurophysiology.

I enjoy working on projects that have potential for dissemination and reach a high standard in both rigor and presentation/user experience.
